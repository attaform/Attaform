# AF02

**The schema declares a kind that has no form representation, so the adapter stops at construction.**

In development this reads:

> [attaform/zod] unsupported kind 'promise' at 'settings.loader'

with the offending kind and its path filled in. The production message carries the same two details after the code.

## What happened

A form value has to round-trip through inputs, serialization, and validation. Attaform carries a value it cannot describe rather than refusing it, so the refused list is short: `z.promise`, `z.function`, `z.map`, `z.symbol`, and `z.templateLiteral`. Anything else is accepted, including kinds Attaform has no structural knowledge of (`z.instanceof(File)`, `z.custom<T>()`, `z.unknown()`, `z.any()`), which are held as opaque leaves and validated by their own predicate.

`z.promise` and `z.function` are refused because they are not values: a Promise is a pending box nothing can bind to, and a callback is behaviour rather than form state. The other three are representable in principle and simply are not modelled yet.

## How to fix it

Reshape the field to a representable kind at that path. A `Map` becomes a `z.record(...)` or an array of entries; a template literal becomes a `z.string()` with a `.regex(...)`; a lazily loaded value resolves before it enters the form; a callback lives outside the schema entirely. [The schema contract](/docs/schemas/contract#what-the-adapters-accept) carries the full list for both Zod majors.

If you reached this page trying to model a file input, you do not need a workaround: `z.instanceof(File)` works on both Zod majors, and Zod v4 also ships `z.file()`. See [File inputs](/docs/binding-inputs/file).
