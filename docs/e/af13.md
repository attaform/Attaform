# AF13

**The Zod v3 adapter reached the backstop it keeps for a schema kind its blank walker has no case for, and defaulted that field to `null`.**

In development this reads:

> [attaform] zod-v3 adapter: unsupported schema kind '\<kind>' on form '\<form key>'. Defaulting the field to null. Use a supported zod kind (object/array/record/string/number/etc.) at this path.

The production message carries the same two values after the code. There is no concrete kind to show in their place, for the reason below.

## What happened

Nothing you did, and nothing your schema did. This is a backstop, and no Zod v3 schema reaches it.

Every kind the v3 adapter recognizes has its own case in the walker that derives blank values, and a `typeName` the adapter does not recognize reads as `unknown`, which has a case of its own: no blank is derived and the slot stays absent until something writes to it. That is the opaque carry [AF03](/e/af03) describes, and it is what leaves this backstop with nothing to catch. The Zod v4 adapter wires the same hook to a silent fallback for the same reason.

The advice in the message is older than the behaviour around it. It dates from the era when Attaform kept a list of supported kinds and refused the rest, which is exactly what [AF02](/e/af02) and [AF03](/e/af03) were retired for. Every Zod kind is welcome. The only rule left is that the schema root must hold keys, which is [AF15](/e/af15).

## What to do

Treat a real sighting the way you would [AF11](/e/af11) or [AF12](/e/af12): an Attaform invariant broke, rather than a schema needing a reshape. [Open an issue](https://github.com/attaform/Attaform/issues) with the reported kind and the schema at that path, and leave the field exactly as you wrote it.

[The schema contract](/docs/schemas/contract#what-the-adapters-accept) lists the blank Attaform derives for every kind.
